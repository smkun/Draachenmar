# Draachenmar Timeline and Calendar System

## Calendar System

### Annual Structure
- **Year Length**: 336 days total
- **Month Length**: 28 days per month
- **Months per Year**: 12 months
- **Week Length**: 7 days per week
- **Special Period**: 5-day Festival of Seasons (transition period)

### Months of the Year

1. **Frostwane** - The waning of winter's grip
2. **Snowshroud** - Deep winter, heavy snowfall
3. **Icebloom** - Winter's peak, ice formations bloom
4. **Thawtide** - The spring thaw begins
5. **Greenreach** - Spring growth reaches its peak
6. **Suncrest** - Early summer, sun at its height
7. **Fireblaze** - Midsummer heat and blazing sun
8. **Emberfall** - Late summer, cooling embers
9. **Leafshadow** - Autumn begins, leaves cast shadows
10. **Harvestmoon** - Harvest time under the full moon
11. **Stormgale** - Autumn storms and strong winds
12. **Starfrost** - Winter's return, frost under stars

### Days of the Week

The days of the week reflect the mystical and elemental nature of Draachenmar:

1. **Solarday** - Day of the sun
2. **Moonsday** - Day of the moon
3. **Starday** - Day of the stars
4. **Emberday** - Day of fire and flame
5. **Skyday** - Day of air and wind
6. **Stoneday** - Day of earth and stone
7. **Restday** - Day of rest and reflection

### Festival of Seasons

**Duration**: 5 days
**Timing**: Transition period between Starfrost (12th month) and Frostwane (1st month)
**Purpose**: Celebration marking the changing of seasons
**Activities**: Feasting, merriment, seasonal ceremonies, and various cultural activities

This festival serves as both a new year celebration and a time to honor the cyclical nature of the seasons, connecting deeply with the Court of Seasons pantheon.

## Complete Historical Timeline

### The Ancient Era
- **Year 0 AF (After Fall)** - A fragment of the Centennial comet "the Emissary" breaks off, crashing into Bargothia and causing the Great Wound
- **Year 1 AF** - K'Hara begins her rise to power in the transformed Orc Kettles
- **Year 5 AF** - K'Hara draws a line the Bargothians should not cross in an event known as "the Stacking"
- **Year 6 AF** - Bargothians mass their armies and cross beyond the stack, beginning the great war
- **Year 9 AF** - After three long years of war, Shining Bargothia lies in ruins. The horde reaches Gulanbarak where the Dragon Conclave finally halts their advance at the cost of dragon extinction

### The Age of Recovery
- **Year 259 AF** - Prophecy of Dragons Campaign begins
- **Year 284 AF** - The Abyssal Egg Campaign commences
- **Year 286 AF** - Alliance is forged with the Frost Giants of Vilos
- **Year 287 AF** - With aid from the Frost Giants, the Abyssal Egg is shattered and Terzamog is defeated

### The Modern Era
- **Year 300 AF** - Jungles of Uxmal Campaign
- **Year 301 AF** - Gnomes add a 7th ship to their fleet of airships in Kronus
- **Year 302 AF** - Drow scouts report the rise of a new Warlord in the Orc Kettles
- **Year 305 AF** - The lost city of Parlainth returns to the shores of the Shattered Sea
- **Year 306 AF** - The town of Haven is established near Parlainth; adventures begin exploring the ruined metropolis
- **Year 307 AF** - Echoes of Madness Campaign

## Dating System Notes

- **AF (After Fall)**: The primary dating system used throughout Draachenmar, marking years since the comet fragment's impact
- **The Fall**: Refers specifically to the fall of Shining Bargothia and the catastrophic events of Year 0
- **Current Era**: The timeline suggests ongoing campaigns and adventures continuing beyond Year 307 AF

## Seasonal Connections

The calendar system closely ties to the **Court of Seasons** pantheon:
- **Frostwane to Icebloom**: Hibernus (Winter) season
- **Thawtide to Greenreach**: Vernalis (Spring) season
- **Suncrest to Emberfall**: Aestus (Summer) season
- **Leafshadow to Stormgale**: Foliara (Autumn) season
- **Starfrost**: Transition back to winter

## Cultural Significance

The calendar reflects the world's connection to:
- **Elemental themes**: Days named for fundamental elements
- **Celestial cycles**: Solar, lunar, and stellar references
- **Natural seasons**: Month names reflecting seasonal changes
- **Religious observance**: Festival timing aligned with pantheon worship
- **Agricultural cycles**: Harvest and planting seasons clearly marked

This system provides a complete framework for tracking time, planning campaigns, and understanding the cultural rhythms of Draachenmar.
