# Tribute and Dedication

## Contributors and Acknowledgments

I'd like to extend our heartfelt gratitude to all the people, whose contributions have been instrumental in bringing Draachenmar to life: Anthony Mambuca, Chris Meyer, Bill Natola, Dan Weiner, Joseph Ettl, Mario Smith-Pignone, Mike Amthor, Nick Mambuca, Paul Moore, Alan Hebert, Richard Auffrey, Mike Wypyszinski and to all the 32gamers that have played in Draachenmar over the years.

Your presence and creativity have shaped this world, leaving an indelible mark on the tapestry of Draachenmar. I am honored to have you as friends and can't thank you enough.

As we continue to embark on adventures in Draachenmar, may our shared stories continue to grow and evolve, inspired by the limitless imagination and camaraderie that defines our amazing group.

## In Memory of Our Dear Friend Tony

Without you, Draachenmar would not exist. Your creative genius and boundless enthusiasm have breathed life into this world. You are the guiding force behind our shared dreams, and we owe the existence of this fantastical realm to your vision.
