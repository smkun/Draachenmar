# Gulanbarak, "The Wall"

## Introduction

The Ironhammer family is a symbol of Gulanbarak's strength, unity, and dedication to craftsmanship, defense, and tradition. Together, they ensure the continued prosperity and security of their mountainous realm.

## The Royal Family

### High King Rathgar "Stoneforge" Ironhammer

King Rathgar "Stoneforge" Ironhammer is the revered ruler of Gulanbarak. He earned the moniker "Stoneforge" due to his unparalleled skill in crafting and forging, which has enriched the kingdom's wealth and defensive capabilities. Rathgar is a stout, bearded dwarf with a strong presence, often seen wearing regal armor adorned with intricate, rune-inscribed designs. He wields the legendary waraxe, "Earthshaker," a symbol of his authority and craftsmanship. He rules over the seven dwarven Kings (Bulgad Grimmantle; Ellgrid Earthforge; Naser Graybeard; Baelgin Ironhand; Gromli Steelarm; Velog Stormshield; Hjalmar Anvilheart), all of which guard the massive dwarven wall nation of "Gulanbarak" from the evil hordes of the Shard Lands.

### Queen Kelda Ironhammer

Queen Kelda is King Rathgar's steadfast partner and the heart of Gulanbarak's royal family. Her intelligence and charisma complement Rathgar's leadership, making her an invaluable advisor and diplomat. Kelda is renowned for her exquisite jewelry, crafted from the finest gemstones and precious metals mined deep within the dwarven realm. She is beloved among the dwarves for her benevolent nature and tireless efforts to improve the lives of her subjects.

### Prince Balthyr Ironhammer

Prince Balthyr, the eldest son of King Rathgar and Queen Kelda, is a warrior of exceptional skill and honor. He commands the Ironguard, Gulanbarak's elite defense force, and is known for his unwavering dedication to protecting the kingdom from external threats. Balthyr carries the ancestral warhammer, "Stonebreaker," a symbol of his lineage and commitment to his people.

### Princess Hilda Ironhammer

Princess Hilda is known for her passion for the arcane arts and her role as the kingdom's chief mage. She studies ancient dwarven runic magic and has used her knowledge to strengthen Gulanbarak's defenses with powerful enchantments. Her robes are adorned with runes that shimmer with protective magic, and she is respected as a guardian of dwarven tradition.

### Prince Darrin Ironhammer

Prince Darrin, the youngest member of the Ironhammer family, possesses a keen intellect and a thirst for knowledge. He serves as the kingdom's chief historian and explorer, delving into the depths of Gulanbarak's extensive tunnels to uncover forgotten dwarven lore and hidden treasures. Darrin's discoveries contribute to the kingdom's rich history and cultural heritage.

## Military Forces at The Wall

In the fantasy realm of Draachenmar, the defense of the Wall is a matter of utmost importance, as it safeguards the inhabitants of the realm from the perils that lurk beyond. Many races from across the land have pledged their aid in the defense of this formidable barrier, each contributing their unique strengths and specialties to create a diverse and powerful military force.

### Dwarves

Dwarves, known for their craftsmanship and resilience, play a vital role in the defense of the Wall.

**Ironwatch**
Stationed along the upper walls, the Ironwatch are crossbow specialists renowned for their precision and marksmanship. They provide long-range support and are a formidable force in repelling threats from a distance.

**Ironguard**
Positioned on the lower walls, the Ironguard are skilled in arms and armor, serving as the frontline defenders of the Wall. Their formidable presence and unwavering resolve make them a stalwart shield against any invaders.

**Obsidian Vanguard**
Hailing from the depths of the Underdark, the Obsidian Vanguard specializes in stealth and magic. They operate in the shadows, using their arcane prowess to thwart intruders attempting to breach the Wall's defenses.

### Elves

Elves, with their grace and affinity for nature, bring a blend of magic and martial prowess to the Wall's defense.

**Gladestalkers**
Positioned at various locations along the Wall, the Gladestalkers are a versatile force skilled in both arms and magic. They bring their natural connection to the land to the forefront, ensuring a harmonious defense.

**Sky Talons**
Patrolling the upper walls, the Sky Talons are griffin riders, capable of swift aerial maneuvers. They provide reconnaissance, engage in hit-and-run tactics, and offer a crucial advantage in battles.

### Gnomes

Gnomes, known for their inventiveness, contribute their technological ingenuity to the Wall's defense.

**G.S. Zephyr Serpent**
Guarding the docks, the G.S. Zephyr Serpent is a marvel of gnome engineering. This formidable vessel serves as both a deterrent and a means of defense, ensuring that threats from the sea are met with formidable resistance.

**Arcane Bombardiers**
Positioned on the upper walls, the Arcane Bombardiers operate powerful artillery, raining destruction upon approaching foes. Their devices are a testament to gnome innovation and firepower.

### Halflings

While halflings may not have dedicated military forces stationed at the Wall, their resilience and adaptability often make them invaluable members of mixed-arm units.

### Humans

Humans, diverse and adaptable, contribute both martial and magical might to the Wall's defense.

**Several Mercenary Companies**
Spread across various locations, these mercenary companies offer mixed arms and combat expertise. They provide additional manpower and tactical flexibility to bolster the Wall's defenses.

## Legends and Tales of Gulanbarak

### The Earthshaker's Origin

The legendary waraxe, "Earthshaker", was said to have been forged in the heart of the mightiest volcano of the old world by the first King of Gulanbarak. Legend tells that it was crafted using a fragment of a fallen star, melded with the purest of dwarven metals. It resonates with an aura of power, and when swung in battle, can make the ground tremble, giving it its name.

### The Tale of Queen Kelda's Crown

A symbol of her authority, Queen Kelda's crown is a marvel to behold. It is said that each gemstone on the crown represents a significant event or victory in Gulanbarak's history. The largest gem, a radiant sapphire, symbolizes the unity of the seven dwarven kings under the Ironhammer rule.

### The Ironguard's Valor

Prince Balthyr once led the Ironguard into a fierce battle against the dark creatures of the Shard Lands. With their combined might and Balthyr's leadership, they were able to push back the invasion, sealing a critical gateway into the Shard Lands. This tale is often sung by minstrels during great feasts, inspiring young warriors.

### The Runic Codex

Hidden deep within Gulanbarak's libraries lies the Runic Codex, a tome of immense magical power. It's said that Princess Hilda, with her profound understanding of runic magic, was the first in centuries to decipher its cryptic pages, unlocking secrets that have bolstered the kingdom's defenses.

### Darrin's Unearthed Relic

On one of his expeditions, Prince Darrin discovered a lost city deep within the bowels of the mountains. In it, he found an ancient relic, a staff said to be wielded by the first mages of Gulanbarak. The staff, now displayed in the kingdom's grand museum, is a testament to Gulanbarak's long and storied history.

## Celebrations and Festivities

### The Festival of Forges

Celebrated annually, this festival honors King Rathgar's craftsmanship. Dwarves from all over Gulanbarak gather to showcase their forging skills, create masterpieces, and pay tribute to the legacy of "Stoneforge."

### Gemstone Gala

Hosted by Queen Kelda, the gala is a night of dancing, music, and admiration of the finest jewelry crafted in the kingdom. It's a night where dwarves exchange stories of their mining exploits and the origins of their precious gemstones.

### Defender's Day

A day to honor the valor of the Ironguard and Prince Balthyr's leadership. Young dwarves train in combat, and the tales of Gulanbarak's mightiest battles are retold.

### Arcane Assembly

Held in the grand halls of the kingdom, this is where mages, led by Princess Hilda, gather to share knowledge, discuss magical theories, and demonstrate their prowess in the arcane arts.

### Expedition Eve

A celebration of discovery, inspired by Prince Darrin's adventures. Dwarves share tales of their explorations, discoveries, and the mysteries of the deep tunnels.

Through these legends, tales, and celebrations, the legacy of the Ironhammer family and the spirit of Gulanbarak lives on, ever reminding the dwarves of their rich history, unity, and the might of their kingdom.

## Districts of Gulanbarak and Their Leaders

### Durinhold
**Leader:** King Bulgad Grimmantle

The very core of Gulanbarak, Durinhold is the oldest and most revered part of the city. It houses the ancient shrines, temples, and the royal palace of the Ironhammer family. Rich in history, its walls are adorned with ancestral carvings depicting the legacy of the dwarven people.

### Thrainforge
**Leader:** King Ellgrid Earthforge

Thrainforge is the pulsing heart of industry in Gulanbarak. The district's forges never sleep, with constant crafting, smelting, and blacksmithing. It's a hub for traders, artisans, and anyone looking to acquire or sell authentic dwarven craftsmanship.

### Morndrak Depths
**Leader:** King Naser Graybeard

Dedicated to the art of mining and excavation, Morndrak Depths features a complex network of tunnels that delve deep into the mountain's heart. Here, precious gemstones and metals are unearthed, illuminated by the luminescent glow of discovered crystals.

### Nebelgar Heights
**Leader:** King Baelgin Ironhand

Situated at the mountain's higher reaches, Nebelgar Heights oversees the external defenses and watchtowers of Gulanbarak. A strategic vantage point, the district is perpetually shrouded in mist, offering both beauty and an added layer of protection against potential threats.

### Runicar Spire
**Leader:** King Gromli Steelarm

A haven for the learned and magical, Runicar Spire is home to the city's grand libraries, arcane academies, and the council of elder mages. The district exudes an air of quiet reverence, with streets alive with the soft murmurs of runes and enchantments.

### Stoneharbor
**Leader:** King Velog Stormshield

Positioned at the mountain's base, Stoneharbor is Gulanbarak's face to the outer world. With its maze-like canals and bustling docks, it's the center of trade and commerce. Dwarven ships, intricately carved with protective runes, set sail from here, bridging Gulanbarak with far-off lands and cultures. Amidst the market's clamor, one can hear tales from distant shores, whispered between traders over mugs of frothy ale.

### Valthor Hollow
**Leader:** King Hjalmar Anvilheart

A sanctuary of wisdom, Valthor Hollow is where Gulanbarak's rich history is chronicled and preserved. Tranquil and serene, this district houses the city's most ancient and knowledgeable inhabitants. Grand libraries, with shelves reaching the ceilings, hold scrolls and tomes dating back centuries. Elders gather in quiet councils, discussing matters of lore and legacy, ensuring the tales of old are passed down through generations.
